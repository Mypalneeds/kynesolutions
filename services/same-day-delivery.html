The basics

What is Kyne same-day delivery?

Kyne same-day delivery means your parcel is picked up and delivered to your customer on the same day. It covers all of Lagos and the Ogun State border towns in our network, for parcels picked up from your location or shipped from Kyne warehousing.

In Lagos, customers compare. When a parcel arrives the same day, it builds trust in your brand and makes repeat orders far more likely.

What is included

  • Pickup from your home, shop, market or our warehouse
  • Delivery to homes, offices and estates
  • Coverage across Lagos Island and Mainland
  • Ogun border towns including Sango-Ota, Ifo, Mowe, Arepo and Agbara
  • Riders who know Lagos routes and traffic
  • Booking through kyneparcel.com
