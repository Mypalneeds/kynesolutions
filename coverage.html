How coverage works

One network, every address.

We have dedicated pages for 20 parts of Lagos and 5 Ogun border areas, but our coverage is not limited to this list. If your customer is anywhere in Lagos State, Kyne can deliver.

The Ogun State border towns we serve sit along the Lagos-Abeokuta, Lagos-Ibadan and Lagos-Badagry expressways, where many people live in Ogun but work and shop in Lagos.
