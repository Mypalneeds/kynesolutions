What we do

Everything after the sale, handled.

Online selling is the easy part. Packing, storing, finding a reliable rider and making sure the customer actually receives the order is where vendors lose time and money. Kyne takes that work off your hands.

01 / Service

Order fulfillment

We receive your orders, pick and pack them properly, and hand them to a rider for delivery, so you can keep selling.

How it works

From your shop to their door in four steps.

Step 01

Create your account

Sign up on kyneparcel.com in a few minutes and add your business and pickup details.

Step 02

Book or send stock

Book a pickup for each order, or bring your stock into Kyne warehousing so we ship from our shelves.

Step 03

We pick, pack and ride

A Kyne rider collects the order, or our team packs it, and heads straight to your customer.

Step 04

Your customer receives it

The parcel is handed over the same day across Lagos and the Ogun border towns we serve.
