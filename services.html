Services

Logistics services that work as one.

Three services that work together: order fulfillment, warehousing and same-day delivery. Use one, or let Kyne run your logistics from shelf to doorstep.

Quick answer

Kyne offers three e-commerce logistics services in Lagos: order fulfillment (pick, pack and dispatch), warehousing (stock storage in Lagos) and same-day delivery across Lagos and the Ogun State border towns.

01 / Order fulfillment

Order fulfillment

We receive your orders, pick and pack them properly, and hand them to a rider for delivery, so you can keep selling.

  • Receiving and checking your orders
  • Picking the right items from your stock in our warehouse
  • Packing that protects your products and your brand
  • Dispatch with a trained Kyne rider
Explore order fulfillment
02 / Warehousing

Warehousing

Keep your stock with us in Lagos, close to your customers, so orders ship from our shelves instead of your living room.

  • Storage for your products in Lagos
  • Stock kept organised and ready to ship
  • Direct link to order fulfillment
  • Same-day delivery from our shelves
Explore warehousing
03 / Same-day delivery

Same-day delivery

Pickup and drop-off on the same day across all of Lagos and the Ogun State border towns.

  • Pickup from your home, shop, market or our warehouse
  • Delivery to homes, offices and estates
  • Coverage across Lagos Island and Mainland
  • Ogun border towns including Sango-Ota, Ifo, Mowe, Arepo and Agbara
