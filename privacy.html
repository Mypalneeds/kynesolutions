Privacy policy

Plain-language terms for using Kyne Solutions and Kyne Parcel.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Kyne Solutions ("Kyne", "Kyne Parcel", "we") collects and uses personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or use our delivery, fulfillment and warehousing services. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, business name, phone number and email address.
  • Delivery information, such as pickup and drop-off addresses and recipient names and phone numbers, needed to complete deliveries.
  • Messages you send us by phone, email, WhatsApp or social media.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider or analytics tools if used.

How we use it

  • To pick up, store, pack and deliver orders.
  • To contact you or your customers about a delivery.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide support.
  • To improve our services and this website.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing

We share delivery details only with the Kyne team members and riders who need them to complete a delivery, and with service providers who help us run our business. We do not sell personal information.

Booking platform

Bookings are made on kyneparcel.com. Information you enter there is used to provide our services and is handled in line with this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide our services, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Contact us at kynesolutions@gmail.com or 0915 178 2751.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
