Our services

Kyne provides e-commerce logistics in Lagos State and selected Ogun State border towns, including same-day delivery, order fulfillment and warehousing. Bookings are made on kyneparcel.com or by contacting our team.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate pickup and delivery details, including a reachable recipient phone number.
  • Package items properly unless Kyne is packing them under a fulfillment arrangement.
  • Do not send illegal, dangerous or prohibited items. Contact us before sending fragile, perishable, oversized or high-value items.
  • Pay applicable charges as shown at booking or as agreed with Kyne.

Delivery times

We work to deliver on the same day across our coverage area. Delivery times can be affected by traffic, weather, incorrect details, recipient availability and other events outside our control.
