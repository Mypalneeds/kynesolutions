About the area

Delivering in Victoria Island

Victoria Island is the business heart of Lagos, with corporate offices, banks, hotels and restaurants packed along Adeola Odeku, Ahmadu Bello Way and Ajose Adeogun.

It falls under Eti-Osa and is fully covered by the Kyne Lagos delivery network. Customers on VI are often at work during the day, so office deliveries and handovers to front desks and receptions are a big part of moving orders here.

Whether you are a vendor based in Victoria Island or you sell to customers who live here, Kyne gives you one dependable partner for fulfillment, warehousing and same-day delivery.
