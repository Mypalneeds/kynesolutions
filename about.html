About Kyne

The delivery partner built for Lagos e-commerce.

Kyne Parcel was founded in Lagos on 2 July 2019 by Mr. Adeitan Ogunmikan with one goal: give e-commerce vendors fast and efficient delivery they can rely on.

Quick answer

Kyne Solutions (Kyne Parcel) is a Lagos-based e-commerce logistics company founded on 2 July 2019 by Adeitan Ogunmikan. It provides order fulfillment, warehousing and same-day delivery across Lagos and the Ogun State border towns.

Get started Talk to our team
Our story

Started in Lagos, for Lagos sellers.

Online businesses in Lagos were growing fast, but getting orders to customers was slow, unpredictable and stressful.

Kyne Parcel was established on 2 July 2019 to fix that. Our founder, Mr. Adeitan Ogunmikan, set out to build an e-commerce delivery company that provides fast and efficient delivery in Lagos, run by people who understand what it means to promise a customer their order and then keep that promise.

Since then Kyne has grown from delivery into a full e-commerce logistics partner. Today we offer order fulfillment, warehousing and same-day delivery across all of Lagos and the Ogun State border towns.

We call ourselves your e-commerce logistics bro because that is the relationship we want with every vendor: someone dependable who has your back when orders come in.

What we stand for

The three things we will not compromise on.

Consistency

Reliable service every single day, so your customers have the same good experience every time they order from you.

Wide delivery coverage

Every part of Lagos, plus the Ogun border towns close to the city. You should never have to turn a customer away because of their address.

Warehousing

Storage that sits right inside our delivery operation, so stock moves from shelf to customer with fewer steps.
