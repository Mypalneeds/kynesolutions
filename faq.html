About Kyne

Kyne Solutions, also known as Kyne Parcel, is an e-commerce delivery and logistics company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We help online vendors with order fulfillment, warehousing and same-day delivery.

Kyne Parcel was founded on 2 July 2019 in Lagos, Nigeria, by Mr. Adeitan Ogunmikan, to provide fast and efficient delivery for e-commerce businesses in Lagos.

Yes. Kyne Solutions is the company and Kyne Parcel is our delivery platform and service brand. You book deliveries on kyneparcel.com.

Three things: consistency, wide delivery coverage across Lagos and the Ogun border towns, and warehousing that lets us fulfil orders straight from our shelves. We focus only on e-commerce, so we understand how online vendors work.

For vendors

Kyne is built for e-commerce vendors of every size: Instagram, WhatsApp and TikTok sellers, online stores, and growing brands that ship orders to customers in Lagos and nearby Ogun State.

Go to kyneparcel.com and click Get started to create your account. You can then book pickups and deliveries. Our team is available on 0915 178 2751 if you want help setting up.

No. Whether you send one parcel a week or many orders a day, you can book deliveries with Kyne. As you grow, you can add fulfillment and warehousing.

Delivery rates depend on pickup and drop-off locations and the service you choose. You can see the rate for your delivery when you book on kyneparcel.com, or call us on 0915 178 2751 for a quote.

Delivery

Yes. Same-day delivery is available across Lagos and the Ogun State border towns we serve. Book early in the day to give your order the best chance of arriving the same day.

Book on kyneparcel.com with your pickup address, your customer's delivery address and the item details. A Kyne rider is assigned to pick up and deliver.

Log in to your Kyne Parcel account on kyneparcel.com to follow your deliveries, or contact our team on 0915 178 2751 for an update.

We deliver the products online vendors sell every day, such as fashion, beauty, gadgets and accessories, home goods and packaged food. Please contact us before sending anything fragile, oversized, high-value or restricted so we can advise you.

Fulfillment and warehousing

Order fulfillment means we handle the work between a customer placing an order and receiving it: picking the right item, packing it properly and getting it delivered. You focus on selling.

Yes. With Kyne warehousing your stock is kept in Lagos and shipped directly from us when orders come in, which saves you time and trips.

Contact our team on 0915 178 2751 or kynesolutions@gmail.com to tell us about your products and volumes. We will explain how to bring in your stock and how orders will be fulfilled.

Coverage

Yes. Kyne delivers across all of Lagos, both the Island and the Mainland, from Lekki and Ajah to Ikeja, Ikorodu, Festac, Ojo and Alimosho.

We deliver to Ogun State border towns close to Lagos, including Sango-Ota, Ifo, Akute, Mowe, Ibafo, Arepo and Agbara.
